Footsteps of the Gospel

The Gospels and Acts, from the Annunciation to Paul in Rome, and the apostles after · c. 7 BC to AD 95 · Scripture: RSV-2CE

    This journeyRecent journeys, or legendRoutes follow the main roads; Scripture rarely names them
    Dates are approximate. Herod died around 4 BC, so Jesus' birth is usually placed about 6 to 4 BC; the ministry runs from c. AD 29 to the Passover of AD 33, and Acts from Pentecost to Paul's house arrest in Rome, c. AD 60–62. The last lane, after Acts, follows Mary and the apostles by tradition; each event says whether it rests on Scripture, early tradition or later legend, and legendary journeys are dashed. The Gospel order follows A. T. Robertson's Harmony of the Gospels (1922). Use the arrow keys to step.
